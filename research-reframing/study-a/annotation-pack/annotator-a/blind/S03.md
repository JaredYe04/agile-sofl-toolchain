# S03

## Requirements

Members book a tennis court up to 7 days ahead today. Change new bookings so they can be made up to 14 days ahead. The source text does not say what happens to a booking that was placed under the old limit.

## Informal specification

```markdown
# Functions

## Place a booking

A member places a court booking. New bookings may be made up to 14 days ahead. Today the limit is 7 days.

## Charge a no-show

A member who misses a booked court is charged a no-show fee. This change does not alter that fee.

# Data Resources

## Member

The member records the membership number and the name.

# Constraints

## New advance window

A new booking may be placed up to 14 days ahead. The source text does not say what happens to a booking that was placed under the old limit.
```

## Candidate slot prompts

Each prompt names a decision to label. It does not state an answer.

### Slot `placed-booking`

What happens to a booking that was placed under the 7-day limit?
