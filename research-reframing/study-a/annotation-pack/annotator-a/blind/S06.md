# S06

## Requirements

A monthly wash plan costs 20 dollars today. Change the plan price to 25 dollars for renewals after the change date. The source text does not say whether the month already on the books keeps its price.

## Informal specification

```markdown
# Functions

## Renew a plan

A member renews a monthly wash plan. Renewals after the change date cost 25 dollars. Today the plan costs 20 dollars.

## Sell a single wash

The site sells one wash with no plan. This change does not alter the single-wash price.

# Data Resources

## Vehicle
```
