# S13

## Requirements

A filled prescription waits at the pharmacy counter. The source text does not say who is allowed to take it away, and it does not say which proof the counter accepts.

## Informal specification

```markdown
# Functions

## Release a prescription

The counter releases a filled prescription. Who may take it away is not stated.

## Check identity

The counter checks identity before the release. Which proof is accepted is not stated.

# Data Resources

## Prescription

The prescription records the patient name, the medicine, and the ready time.

# Constraints

## Ready status

Only a prescription marked ready may leave the counter.
```

## Candidate slot prompts

Each prompt names a decision to label. It does not state an answer.

### Slot `who-collects`

Who is allowed to take a filled prescription away from the counter?

### Slot `id-proof`

Which proof does the counter require before it releases the medicine?
