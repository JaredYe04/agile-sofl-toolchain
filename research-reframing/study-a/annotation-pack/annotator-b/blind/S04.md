# S04

## Requirements

The drop-off charge for one carload of mixed recyclables is 5 dollars today. Change that charge to 8 dollars for carloads that arrive after the change date. The source text does not say what happens to a punch card that was sold before the change.

## Informal specification

```markdown
# Functions

## Accept a carload

The site accepts one carload of mixed recyclables. Carloads that arrive after the change date pay 8 dollars. Today the charge is 5 dollars.

## Publish hours

The site publishes its open hours. This change does not alter the hours.

# Data Resources

## Site

The site records the address and the open hours.
```
