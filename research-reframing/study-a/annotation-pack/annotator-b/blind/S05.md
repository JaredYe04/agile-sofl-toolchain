# S05

## Requirements

An owner leaves a pet at a kennel overnight and may leave a packet of medicine with the staff. The source text does not say whether the staff give that medicine.

## Informal specification

```markdown
# Functions

## Board a pet

An owner leaves a pet overnight. A packet of medicine may be left with the staff. Whether the staff give that medicine is not stated.

# Data Resources

## Stay

The stay records the pet, the owner, and the night.

# Constraints

## Recorded stay

A boarded pet has one open stay.
```

## Candidate slot prompts

Each prompt names a decision to label. It does not state an answer.

### Slot `give-medicine`

Do kennel staff give medicine that the owner left with them?
